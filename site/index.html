Build a whole city by solving math problems.

A free math game for kids aged 6 to 14.

In Math City, kids build their own town by answering math questions: a first little house, then shops, schools, parks and streets that light up at night. Townspeople send letters asking for what they need next, and it's up to them to build it.

The questions adapt to each child. There are over 350 skills from kindergarten to grade 8, including counting, addition and subtraction, times tables, fractions, decimals, measurement, geometry and early algebra. New topics appear as earlier ones are mastered. Questions can be read aloud, and longer ones come with a scratch pad for working out.

Several kids can share one device, each with their own player, avatar and city. The game works offline, has no ads or purchases, and collects no data. Everything stays on the device.

  • Ages 6 to 14
  • iPad, iPhone or Android
  • Free, no ads
  • Works offline
  • Collects no data
  • Several kids per device

A city that's all their own

What playing looks like

  • Real diagrams Clocks, protractors, number lines, graphs.

  • Pictures first The picture carries the idea, then the numbers.

  • A scratch pad Longer problems get paper to work on.
