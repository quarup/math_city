# Math City Privacy Policy

*Effective 20 October 2026*

Math City is a math practice game for children. It is built to collect
nothing about the children who play it, or about anyone else.

## The short version

- **We collect no data.** The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else.
- **Everything stays on the device.** Players, progress and cities are saved
  only on the iPad, iPhone or Android device the app is installed on.
- **No ads, no tracking, no analytics.** The app contains no advertising,
  no analytics or tracking tools, and no third-party services that collect
  data.
- **No accounts and no internet needed.** The app works fully offline. It
  has no sign-in, no chat and no way for players to contact each other.

## What the app stores on the device

To let several children share one device, the app saves the following
**only on that device**:

- each player's name (any name the child or parent chooses, which need not
  be a real name), avatar and school grade;
- their progress: the math skills they have practiced and how well they
  know them, and the city they have built;
- app settings, such as whether questions are read aloud.

None of this is sent anywhere by the app. It is removed when the app is
deleted from the device.

Your device's own backup, if you have turned it on (for example iCloud
Backup or Google device backup), may include the app's saved data, in the
same way it backs up other apps. That backup is handled by Apple or Google
under their privacy policies and your settings, not by us.

## Read-aloud

When read-aloud is on, questions are spoken using the speech engine built
into your device. The app does not record audio and never uses the
microphone.

## Children's privacy

Math City is designed for children aged 6 to 14. Because the app collects
no personal information, we have nothing to store, share or sell, and no
information for parents to request or delete from us. Parents can see
each player's progress in the app, and can remove all of it by deleting
the app.

## Beta testing through TestFlight

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple shares
some information with us as part of TestFlight, such as the email address
you were invited with, crash reports and any feedback or screenshots you
choose to send. We use this only to fix and improve the app, and Apple
handles it under its own privacy policy. This applies to TestFlight
testers only, not to the app itself.

## Changes

If a future version of the app ever changes what it stores or sends, for
example an optional cloud save through Apple Game Center or Google Play
Games, we will update this page before that version is released and
change the date at the top.
