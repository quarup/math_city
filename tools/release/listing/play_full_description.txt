Build a whole city by solving math problems!

In Math City, kids build their own town by answering math questions: a first little house, then shops, schools, parks and streets that light up at night. Townspeople send letters asking for what they need next, and it's up to you to build it.

★ Over 350 skills from kindergarten to grade 8
Counting, addition and subtraction, times tables, fractions, decimals, measurement, time, geometry, data and early algebra.

★ Adapts to each child
Questions start at your child's grade. New topics appear as earlier ones are mastered, and old favourites come back for review.

★ Made to be understood
Real diagrams — clocks, protractors, number lines, fraction bars and graphs. Questions can be read aloud for early readers, and longer problems come with a scratch pad for working out. A wrong answer shows how to get it right.

★ One device, the whole family
Several kids can each have their own player, avatar, progress and city.

★ Safe and private
Free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no tracking. Math City works fully offline and collects no data: everything stays on the device.

Math City is open source. Questions or feedback: quarup.mc@gmail.com
