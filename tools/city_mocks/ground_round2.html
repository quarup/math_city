Eighteen mocks answering the round-one feedback: the town's own ground made as pleasant as the countryside around it, the city edge shown only while a building is being placed or moved, the haze alone with nothing floating in it, and an Expand city mode that marks land with stakes and string instead of signs. What I would build, in order

  1. R5 + H1 — tended ground with street trees and flower beds inside, density-as-distance outside, and the haze alone. The town and the countryside become one landscape; the checkerboard, the cross and the flat backdrop all go.
  2. B1 + B4 — the edge, the tile grid and the dimmed outside appear only while something is in the hand, for placing and for moving alike. One code path.
  3. E1 + E6 — the Expand city button, stakes and string with a price pill per block, the camera pulling back to frame the ring. E5 is the no-room path into the same mode, replacing round one's letter-and-sign version.
  4. H2 — the eight-minute day carried by the haze colour and the tints alone; H3's stars are yours to allow or not.
  5. Then, as taste allows: E4's preview on selection, B3's grid halo, R3 / R4 if the youngest players turn out to need countable tiles.
