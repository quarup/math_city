Math City Ground and Sky

Twenty-three mocks for how the land under the city and the sky above it should look, how purchasable land announces itself without a pale cross, and what a brand-new player sees in their first ten minutes. Live on the real sprites at the game's 64 px tile; every idea from the brainstorm is here, including the ones I would not build.

Artevery mock on this page is code-drawn in Flame canvas; no Nano Banana sheet is needed for any of it. EffortS under half a day · M one or two sessions. PerfFree is a few draw calls · Light is per-tile or per-entity work worth measuring once on a low-end phone. Tiermy recommendation: Recommend, Nice (after the recommended set), Decide where it is a taste or economy call for you, Reject shown so it is not re-derived.

What I would build, in order

  1. T2 + T4 + S1 — meadow beyond the lawn, the ground dissolving into a viewport-fixed sky. Removes the green square, the cross and the flat backdrop in one change, and gives the day cycle somewhere to live.
  2. S2 + S4 — the eight-minute day with the clock frozen at 9:30 through chapter one, five minutes of day and three of night after the hand-over, paused under the question screens.
  3. F1 + F2 + F5 — no frontier until the land beat; then one sign beside the site that did not fit; after the first purchase, a sign per block. Survey stakes (F3) become the selected state.
  4. N3 — the land letter, with the animated hand on the sign and on Buy. Whether the first deed is a gift (N4) is your call.
  5. N1 + N2 + N5 — start zoom, the tighter clamp, and the disclosure flags on the player row.
  6. Then, as taste allows: T3 density rings, S3 city glow, T6 cloud shadows, F4 the hedge, F6 prices on signs, F7 a Land folder as the home of the price list.

Everything lives in lib/game/city/ and lib/presentation/city/: the meadow and signs are branches in CityBoardComponent's terrain and overlay passes, the sky is a gradient behind the GameWidget plus a viewport-space haze component, and the schedule is a handful of flags on the chapter-one integer that already exists. No schema change except the clock, if D3 is ever chosen over the ambient loop.

Sprites are the shipped assets/buildings/*.png; the world is the signed 4×4 block model of land_blocks.dart on a 15×15-block grid. Canvases pause when scrolled out of view. Companion to Math City Alive.
