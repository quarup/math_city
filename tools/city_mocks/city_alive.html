Math City Alive

Twenty-nine ways to make the isometric city move, each running live on the real building and road sprites at the game's own 64 px tile, with an honest note on what it costs and who makes the art.

Artwho supplies the pixels: I draw it in Flame canvas code, a NB sheet you generate in Nano Banana, or anchors I place once on an existing sprite. EffortS under half a day · M one or two sessions · L several sessions. PerfFree is a few draw calls · Light is per-entity work at tens of entities · Watch means measure on a low-end phone. Tiermy recommendation: Ship first, Nice, Later, or Decide where it is a design call for you.

What I would build, in order

  1. D4 + D2 or D3 — night windows with a day cycle. One pipeline step, one tint pass, and the whole existing catalogue changes character. Pick the clock driver; the render is identical.
  2. C1 + C3 + B4 — birds, cloud shadows, citizen bubbles. Under a day total, no art, and the city stops looking like a diagram.
  3. A1 — flat cars on the road graph. This is the mover system everything else rides on; swapping in A2 sprites later is a drawing change only.
  4. F1 — the working construction site. Ties motion to answering questions.
  5. B1 then A5, D5, G1, G2 — pedestrians, night driving, lamps, tap reactions, idle drift, each reusing the movers and the tint.
  6. E1 to E5 — landmark touches as time allows, one anchor session each.

Everything above sits in lib/game/city/ behind the existing CityBoardComponent render pass; nothing touches the domain layer or the schema except D3's single persisted hour. Motion pauses during the construction zoom and the wheel, so nothing competes with placement or the question.

Sprites are the shipped assets/buildings/*.png; road autotiling and the sprite anchor rule are ports of road_sprites.dart and CityBoardComponent._drawSprite. Canvases pause when scrolled out of view.
