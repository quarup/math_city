Math City Walkers

Thirty-one takes on the code-drawn citizen, each walking the real sidewalk in front of the real sprites. The big view is 2.4× the city zoom; the strip along the bottom is 1×, what a kid sees at the default camera; the corner is a 4× turnaround of the four headings plus standing still.

GroupsProportions, then how the body is built, then the walk cycle, then size, then who is in the crowd, then colour. Mix and match: any body takes any walk. Where I would land

Body: P03 proportions (four heads, tapered legs, feet) with P10's jointed limbs, at 16 to 17 px. It fixes all three complaints at once: the head is an eighth of the height, the torso is narrower than the shoulders, and the legs are thicker than the arms.

Walk: P17 knees and heel lift for the close zoom, since the construction zoom brings the camera to about 2.5× where straight legs look like scissors. At 1× it degrades gracefully into P15's pendulum.

Line and colour: a thin darker-of-fill outline (P31's treatment) rather than black ink, so the figures stay crisp at 1× without the cartoon contrast of P12 against the painterly buildings. Shirts from the saturated set, with a few muted ones mixed in.

Crowd: P25's three ages and P28's pairs from the start, P26 props and P27 dogs as a second pass. Gait variety is what stops a sidewalk looking like a conveyor belt.

Renderer is one function, drawPed(ctx, x, y, dir, phase, look, style); every mock is a style object. Porting to Dart is a straight translation of canvas calls to Canvas/Path.
